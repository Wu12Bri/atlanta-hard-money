How to use this directory

Treat this page as a shortlist builder, not a rate sheet. Hard money and private money terms move with the deal: experience, ARV accuracy, exit plan, lien position, and property type all change what you are actually offered.

  1. Filter by strategy — flip, bridge, construction, or rental/DSCR.
  2. Prefer clear published terms — and call anyone who leaves LTV blank if the deal is time-sensitive.
  3. Open the source link — confirm the number yourself; sites change.
  4. Ask the same questions of every lender — max leverage on purchase vs rehab, draw process, points, prepayment, and who funds (direct vs broker).
  5. Verify licensing and entity details for Georgia before wiring deposits or appraisal fees.

Red flags

  • Upfront fees before underwriting — especially “application” or “processing” fees demanded before a term sheet you can review.
  • Pressure seminars or “act today” pitches that push education products harder than the loan itself.
  • Vague “we fund anything” claims with no geography, property type, or leverage bounds.
  • Broker posing as a direct lender without disclosing that your file will be shopped.
  • Requests for wire details via unsolicited email/text — always confirm using a phone number from the lender’s official site.

FAQ

What is the difference between hard money and private money?

In practice the labels overlap. Both are asset-based, short-term loans secured by investment real estate rather than W-2 income. “Hard money” often means higher leverage rehab/flip capital with points and interest-only payments. “Private money” sometimes implies a direct or relationship lender. Ask who funds the loan and how draws work — those answers matter more than the marketing label.

Do I need perfect credit to get a hard money loan in Atlanta?

Not always. Several local lenders publish “no minimum credit” or asset-based underwriting. Others cite floors (for example 620 or 640) on certain products. Credit still affects pricing and leverage. Confirm the product you need — flip vs DSCR rental programs differ.

What does “70% ARV” actually mean?

After-repair value (ARV) is the expected market value once work is finished. A 70% ARV cap means total loan proceeds (purchase + rehab, depending on structure) generally should not exceed 70% of that finished value. Always ask whether the percentage applies to purchase price, total project cost (LTC), or ARV — lenders mix these terms.

How fast can these loans close in Georgia?

Published claims on this page range from roughly 48 hours to about 7–14 days once documents are complete. Title work, entity docs, insurance, and draw setup often drive the real timeline. Build buffer into your contract.

Is this an endorsement of any lender?

No. Inclusion means we found a public website describing Georgia/Atlanta lending — not that we audited financials, licenses, or borrower reviews. Do your own diligence.
